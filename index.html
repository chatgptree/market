Sector Rotation

Money is rotating out of commodity-linked and cyclical plays as oil and copper slide, and into rate-sensitive growth and quality software names that benefit from the falling 10Y yield. AI infrastructure remains the consensus overweight but selectivity is increasing as valuations diverge sharply between profitable compounders and speculative names.

Key Risk

The primary near-term risk is a hard landing signal embedded in simultaneous oil and copper declines, which could trigger an earnings estimate revision cycle that hits AI infrastructure capex commitments faster than consensus expects. A secondary risk is that the 10Y yield at 4.402% remains elevated enough to compress multiples on high-PEG names if the Fed delays cuts further or inflation re-accelerates.

Macro Shift

The single most important macro development today is that oil breaking below 70 dollars on Hormuz transit resumption threatens to deflate the energy risk premium that has been the anchor of the stagflation and gold-supercycle theses simultaneously, as falling energy CPI in June data could give the Fed political cover to pause any hike and allow risk-on to resume. However the structural damage to global supply chains, the confirmed 4.2 percent headline CPI with core still at 2.9 percent, and the absence of any signed permanent Hormuz agreement mean the thesis pillars are bent rather than broken, and a single re-escalation event would snap risk sentiment back violently.
